About MicCheck

Simple microphone testing for everyone

MicCheck is a free browser-based tool designed to help people check whether their microphone is working properly.

You can view live audio activity, select an available microphone, record a short voice sample, and play it back.

Our approach

We aim to make microphone testing simple, accessible, and easy to understand. The tool processes audio locally in your browser and does not upload your recording to our website.
