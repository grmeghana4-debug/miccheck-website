Why Is My Microphone Not Working? 10 Common Problems and Fixes

A microphone that suddenly stops working can interrupt meetings, online classes, interviews and voice recordings. The cause may be something simple, such as a muted microphone or blocked browser permission.

This guide explains ten common microphone problems and practical troubleshooting steps for computers, headsets and web browsers.

1. Check Whether Your Microphone Is Muted

Start with the simplest check. Look for a physical mute switch on your headset or microphone. Also check the mute button in your meeting application and operating system.

2. Select the Correct Input Device

Your computer may be using a different microphone from the one you intended to use. Open your sound settings and check the selected input device. Select your built-in microphone, USB microphone or headset as needed.

3. Allow Microphone Access in Your Browser

Browsers ask for permission before websites can use your microphone. If you previously blocked access, open the website's permissions from your browser's address bar and change microphone access to Allow. Reload the page afterward.

4. Check Your Operating System Permissions

Your operating system may prevent applications from accessing the microphone. Check the privacy or security settings on your computer and make sure microphone access is enabled for the browser or app you are using.

5. Check Your Microphone Volume

If people can barely hear you, your input level may be too low. Open your sound settings, select your microphone and check its input volume. Speak normally while observing the input level.

6. Reconnect Your Headset or USB Microphone

Disconnect your external microphone and connect it again. Try another USB port if available. For a wired headset, check that its plug is fully inserted into the correct audio jack.

7. Close Other Applications Using the Microphone

Another application may be using the microphone or holding an input selection you did not expect. Close unnecessary recording, meeting and voice-chat applications, then test again.

8. Restart Your Browser or Computer

Temporary software problems can sometimes interrupt microphone access. Save your work, close and reopen your browser, and test again. If the problem continues, restart your computer.

9. Check Your Microphone in Another Application

Try the microphone in another trusted application or your operating system's sound settings. If it works elsewhere, the issue may be specific to the original website or application.

10. Check for Device or Hardware Problems

Inspect the cable, connector and microphone for visible damage. If possible, test the microphone on another compatible device. If it fails across multiple devices, the microphone itself may need repair or replacement.

Helpful tip: Change one setting at a time and test again. This makes it easier to identify what actually fixed the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my microphone detected but not recording sound?

Check whether the microphone is muted, whether the correct input device is selected and whether the application has permission to access it.

Why does my microphone work in one app but not another?

Different applications can have separate microphone permissions and input-device settings. Check the settings in the application that is not working.

Can a browser block my microphone?

Yes. A browser can deny microphone access to an individual website. Review that website's permissions and allow access if you trust it.

How can I tell if my microphone is damaged?

Test it on another compatible device and check whether it works in more than one application. Persistent failure may indicate a hardware problem.

Final Thoughts
