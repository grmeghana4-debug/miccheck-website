What Can You Check With MicCheck?

MicCheck is a free online microphone test that helps you check whether your microphone is connected and picking up sound. You can test your input, monitor audio activity and record a short sample without installing software.

Check if your microphone is working

Start the microphone test, allow browser permission and speak. If the audio level meter moves, your microphone is detecting sound.

Test laptop, desktop and headset microphones

Use the microphone selector to choose an available input device. You can test built-in laptop microphones, USB microphones and supported headset microphones.

Test your microphone before a meeting

Check your microphone before joining an online meeting, video call, interview, class or voice chat.

Does MicCheck upload my recordings?

No. Microphone testing and recording playback happen locally in your browser. This page does not upload your audio recordings.

Frequently asked questions

Is this microphone test free?

Yes. You can test your microphone and record a short sample for free.

Is my voice uploaded?

No. This tool processes microphone audio in your browser. The recording is kept locally for playback and is not uploaded by this page.

Why is my microphone not working?

Check your browser permissions, microphone connection, and device input settings. See our troubleshooting guide.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, on supported mobile browsers when microphone permission is allowed.
